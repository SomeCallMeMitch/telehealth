# Karpa Health and Beluga Health

This file tracks two providers that may matter as alternatives or future migration targets.

## Karpa Health

### Why it matters

Karpa appears to be one of the closest small-company competitors to LegUp in the white-label telehealth space, but its public positioning is more ownership-friendly.

### Publicly observed model

Karpa offers multiple participation levels, including affiliate and white-label/full-clinic options. Public materials observed during research showed entry pricing around **$297/month** for certain white-label/full-clinic options.

### Ownership / offboarding posture

Karpa's public materials state themes such as:

- **your patients, your data**;
- customer lists supplied by the client remain client materials;
- offboarding can include export of patient records/contact information;
- no exit fee / no restriction on re-engaging patients has been represented in public marketing;
- the brand can remain merchant of record rather than Karpa.

This appears materially better than LegUp's current public partner-agreement language.

### Important caveat

Do **not** assume every ownership promise applies to every Karpa plan. Before contracting, get written confirmation that the exact plan purchased includes:

1. full patient/customer ownership;
2. export rights;
3. subscription/payment portability if applicable;
4. no non-solicit / no prohibition on re-engaging customers;
5. clear offboarding procedure;
6. reasonable termination notice.

### Product difference versus LegUp

Karpa appears more heavily oriented toward cash-pay telehealth categories such as GLP-1s, peptides, TRT/HRT, hair/skin, and related programs. It has not yet been established that Karpa offers a direct equivalent to LegUp's very inexpensive **primary care + $0 generic prescription membership**.

That means Karpa may be contractually stronger while LegUp may currently be stronger for the specific low-cost membership concept.

## Beluga Health

### Why it matters

Beluga appears to be a more direct **clinical-infrastructure provider** rather than a simple reseller-in-a-box. It may be a realistic migration destination once meaningful volume exists.

LegUp-related legal disclosures reviewed during research identify **Beluga Health, P.A.** as one of the healthcare organizations that can provide clinical services through portions of the broader stack.

### Beluga's public positioning

Beluga markets directly to founders building DTC telehealth brands and says it supports hundreds of brands.

Publicly described division of responsibility:

**Brand/operator handles:**
- brand;
- marketing;
- acquisition;
- customer service;
- pricing.

**Beluga handles:**
- physician/clinician network;
- clinical workflows;
- prescribing;
- compliance;
- pharmacy routing / integrations.

Beluga publicly emphasizes that it does not need to own the consumer marketing relationship and has represented that it does not market to the brand's patients.

### Economics / structure

Beluga is not a $249/month turnkey business-in-a-box. Public materials describe economics more like:

- monthly platform fee;
- per-clinical-visit fees;
- pharmacy/lab pass-through costs;
- pricing determined after scoping.

It also describes multiple implementation paths, including direct API integration and partner-platform models.

### Why it may become important later

If a branded telehealth business reaches meaningful scale, a direct infrastructure relationship could allow us to:

- own the customer relationship more clearly;
- control more of the software experience;
- reduce dependency on a reseller layer;
- negotiate provider/pharmacy economics based on actual volume;
- make the company itself more transferable/valuable.

### Migration hypothesis

Potential progression:

1. validate demand cheaply with LegUp or equivalent;
2. reach roughly 100-250+ recurring members;
3. measure CAC, churn, utilization, support load, family/individual mix;
4. request direct quotes from Beluga and other infrastructure providers using real operating data;
5. migrate only if economics, ownership, compliance, and service levels improve.

Beluga publicly advertises migration/switching support for established telehealth brands, which aligns well with this strategy.
