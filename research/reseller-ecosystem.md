# LegUpRx / NTP Reseller Ecosystem

## Goal

Identify businesses already marketing LegUpRx / National Telehealth Providers services, how they position the offer, what they charge, and whether any evidence of scale can be found.

## Identified examples

### Simple Freakin' Care

Website: https://simplefreakincare.com/

Observed positioning:
- consumer telehealth membership brand;
- strong emphasis on straightforward access and the prescription benefit;
- observed pricing around **$59/month individual** and **$159/month family** for primary care + Rx;
- family plan marketed for up to eight people;
- legal/operational disclosures identify third-party telehealth fulfillment.

Current research has **not** found credible public subscriber/revenue numbers. Its website existence and pricing should not be treated as proof of scale.

Further work:
- identify founders/owners;
- social profiles;
- Meta Ad Library activity;
- Google ad/search presence;
- business registration / age;
- review volume;
- backlinks / organic search footprint;
- any podcasts/interviews/case studies;
- employee count / LinkedIn signals;
- traffic-estimate tools, used cautiously.

### Fit For Duty Rx

Website: https://fitfordutyrx.com/

Observed positioning:
- workforce / working-person angle;
- care memberships plus prescription savings;
- public formulary identifies **BestChoiceRx** as pharmacy network provider;
- useful source for reverse-engineering the prescription benefit.

Further work:
- determine employer/B2B sales emphasis;
- pricing history;
- ad activity;
- customer/review signals;
- whether they recruit sub-partners.

### Drip Hydration & Wellness Bar

Website: https://www.dripwellnessbar.com/

Observed positioning:
- existing wellness business cross-selling telehealth;
- observed pricing included approximately **$53 Primary + Rx**, with lower-cost urgent/behavioral-health options.

This is strategically interesting because it demonstrates a potentially better distribution model than launching a pure telehealth brand: attach the offer to an existing customer base.

### EZE Optimal Health / EZE Élitaire

Website: https://ezeoptimalhealth.com/

Observed positioning:
- broad health/wellness membership offer;
- observed Primary + Rx pricing around **$60/month**;
- markets the large $0-medication formulary.

## Emerging pattern

The reseller ecosystem appears real, but fragmented.

Three common go-to-market patterns are visible:

1. **Standalone consumer telehealth brand**
   - Example: Simple Freakin' Care

2. **Existing wellness/health business adds telehealth**
   - Example: Drip Hydration & Wellness Bar

3. **Elective-medication telehealth storefront**
   - Many managed white-label sites

The second model may be especially attractive for a B2B channel strategy because the partner already owns customer traffic.

## Important unanswered question: who is actually succeeding?

Public websites do not reveal meaningful operating performance.

Key metrics to seek:
- estimated active subscribers;
- monthly traffic;
- paid-ad intensity;
- review count and velocity;
- organic keyword rankings;
- social following and engagement;
- age of domain/business;
- employee count;
- app downloads, if any;
- whether partner has repeatable B2B acquisition.

## Strategic observation

The current visible reseller market does **not** yet show a dominant consumer brand built around the low-cost Primary Care + $0 Rx value proposition. That may indicate opportunity, but it may also indicate difficult CAC or weak consumer awareness. We need performance evidence before drawing conclusions.
